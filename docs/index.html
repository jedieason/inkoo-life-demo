Technology

Color that feels
like paper.

Six pigments give the display its color. Ambient light makes the image visible without a backlight.

Reflective display

E Ink Spectra™ 6 reads best where there’s enough light to view a print.

PercepTone® image processing

Dovery’s in-house image engine tunes whites, shadows, gradients and texture for the color ePaper palette.

Power on refresh

The panel holds a still image without power. Refreshes and standby electronics draw power.
